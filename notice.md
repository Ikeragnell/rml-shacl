## Provenance

The rml-core, rml-cc, rml-fnml, rml-io, and rml-star shapes in this repository
are derived from the RML specification project's SHACL shapes
(https://w3id.org/rml/*), licensed CC BY 4.0, with substantial modifications.

- RML-Core — Maria, Dimou, De Meester, Iglesias-Molina, Chaves-Fraga,
  Van Assche. https://w3id.org/rml/core — doi:10.5281/zenodo.7919848
- RML-CC — Debruyne, Michel, Iglesias-Molina, Van Assche.
  https://w3id.org/rml/cc — doi:10.5281/zenodo.7919852
- RML-FNML — De Meester, Jozashoori, Iglesias-Molina, Van Assche.
  https://w3id.org/rml/fnml — doi:10.5281/zenodo.7919856
- RML-IO — Iglesias-Molina, Van Assche, Maria.
  https://w3id.org/rml/io — doi:10.5281/zenodo.7919850
  (rml-io-registry shapes are also derived from this module.)
- RML-LV — Maria, De Vleeschauwer, Lanti
  https://w3id.org/rml/lv/ — doi:10.5281/zenodo.19055778
